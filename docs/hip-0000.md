# HIP-0000: Hanzo Dō Architectural Framework

## 1. Abstract
This HIP establishes **Hanzo Dō** as the vertically integrated AI operating system. It defines the foundational pillars—**Kata (Harness)**, **Gen (Agent Engine)**, and **Dojo (Sandbox)**—supported by the **Sutra** specification layer, and formalizes the requirements for recursive, evidence-based evolutionary improvement.

## 2. Architectural Pillars
1. **Sutra (Specification)**: The curated, versioned AI OS specification. It acts as the authority for all interfaces, schemas, and conformance requirements.
2. **Kata (Harness)**: The reproducible execution form. Responsible for agent lifecycle, policy enforcement, resource budgeting, supervision, and evaluation triggering.
3. **Gen (Agent Engine)**: The core intelligence loop. Owns reasoning, action selection, tool orchestration, context management, and recovery.
4. **Dojo (Sandbox)**: The execution environment. Provides isolated, reproducible compute, networking, and filesystem access, implemented via `Visor`.

## 3. Evolutionary Fitness Protocol
Every layer of Hanzo Dō is independently evolvable. Progress is governed by two levels of fitness:

### Level 1: Product Fitness
Measures the capability and efficiency of a deployed system (Agent, Harness, or Model):
- **Verified Success**: Task completion under independent evaluation.
- **Cost Efficiency**: Total execution cost per verified success.
- **Latency**: Time to verified completion.
- **Robustness**: Regression rates on private holdout tasks.

### Level 2: Evolutionary Fitness
Measures the system's ability to discover and reproduce improvements:
- **Research Yield ($Y_R$)**: $\Delta H_{\text{verified}} / C_{\text{total\_research}}$.
- **Successor Research Lift ($L_R$)**: $Y_R(\text{successor}) / Y_R(\text{predecessor})$.
- **Discovery Rate**: Qualified improvements per 100 experiments.
- **Transfer Rate**: Generalization across tasks/environments.
- **Reproducibility**: Discoveries verified by independent evaluation.

## 4. Evolutionary Requirements
- **Provenance**: Candidates must maintain lineage to immutable parents via the `Karma` ledger.
- **Isolation**: Research agents propose mutations; they cannot modify production security boundaries or promotion authority.
- **Independent Qualification**: Promotion to production requires independent verification, integrity checks, and a validated rollback path.
- **Population-based Search**: We preserve diverse evolutionary branches to avoid local optima.
