# HIP-1403: Hanzo Science — Autonomous Research Protocol

## Abstract
This HIP formalizes the **Autonomous Scientific Discovery Protocol**, defining how Hanzo Dō agents hypothesize, experiment, replicate, and contribute to verifiable knowledge.

## Protocol
- **Scientific Evidence**: Hypotheses must be falsifiable and simulations/evaluations must be pre-registered.
- **Independence**: Autonomous research processes are bounded by independent review and qualification gates.
- **Replication**: All discovery claims require independent reproduction tests before XP/Reputation awards.
- **Recursive Improvement**: Meta-optimization of the research process itself must be held to the same rigorous fitness metrics as model/harness optimizations.
