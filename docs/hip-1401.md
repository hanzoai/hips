# HIP-1401: Hanzo Evolution — Candidate & Population Protocol

## Abstract
This HIP formalizes the **Recursive Evolution Protocol** for the Hanzo Dō, defining candidate lineage, mutation surfaces, selection criteria, and promotion gates.

## Protocol
- **EvolutionCandidate**: Every experimental change must be defined as a signed artifact with parent lineage.
- **Population Search**: We preserve diverse evolutionary branches; selection is multi-objective (Pareto-frontier: Quality, Cost, Latency, Safety).
- **Independent Qualification**: Promotion requires holdout-verified results and validated rollback paths.
- **Fitness**: Measurements are governed by Level 1 (Product Fitness) and Level 2 (Evolutionary Fitness/Yield).
